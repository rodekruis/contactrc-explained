A contact centre your National Society can set up in days

DEH runs on Amazon Connect. People call or message one number, and your team answers from a browser. Every contact is connected to Community Feedback Management (CFM), the shared feedback platform on EspoCRM, so feedback gets routed, answered and learned from.

People need to reach us, and we need to hear them

When a cash programme reaches tens of thousands of people, or an emergency reaches a million, the questions, complaints and requests that come back decide whether a programme works.

Most National Societies have no reliable way to receive that feedback on the channels people already use, and no fast way to act on it. DEH and CFM close that gap together: DEH to receive contacts, CFM to handle and learn from them.

What DEH does

A real contact centre in the browser. No phones on desks, no software to install.

Voice with a call menu

Callers choose a language and an option, hear opening hours and wait in the right queue. Messages are spoken by a computer voice that you check before go-live.

WhatsApp

People write to the same programme on WhatsApp. Agents can start a conversation with an approved WhatsApp for Business template from the outbound messaging app.

SMS

Send an SMS to mobile numbers, for example a link after a call. If your main number is a landline, you add a second number for outgoing SMS.

One agent workspace

Calls, chats, the customer profile and EspoCRM sit side by side in tabs. Your team logs in with their own Microsoft 365 account.

Nobody tells their story twice

The caller's earlier contacts show on screen. A transcript and an AI summary help agents catch up fast. The summary is a help, so agents check it.

Chats in any language

Incoming chats are translated for the agent, and replies go back in the language the person chose.

Supervisors stay in control

Four switches, any moment: emergency closure, a temporary message before the queue, callback on long waits, and a short survey after the call.

Hours and holidays set in advance

Opening hours and public holidays, such as Christmas or Eid, are set ahead of time. Callers always hear when you open and where else to find help.

One shared feedback platform behind every contact centre

Community Feedback Management (CFM) runs on one shared EspoCRM instance used by many National Societies. Each National Society sees only its own data. DEH plugs into it, so a contact centre is not a separate island of feedback.

What CFM brings

  • Feedback from different channels, from Kobo to face to face, and now from the contact centre, in one place.
  • One data model for everyone: a Primary Feedback Type, sensitivity, priority and follow-up, aligned with IFRC's common feedback framework.
  • Automatic triage: sensitive cases go to a Feedback Supervisor, follow-ups stay open until someone is assigned, simple appreciation closes itself.
  • Automated analysis with Qualitative Feedback Analysis (QFA): classification, summaries and questions over all feedback, with a human in the loop.
  • Release notes for every change, so each National Society knows what changed and whether it needs to act.

How DEH and CFM work together

  • Live in NL
    EspoCRM opens as a tab inside the DEH agent workspace. Agents work in EspoCRM as they are used to, and notes are kept in EspoCRM.
  • In development
    CFM embedded in any Amazon Connect instance, so every new National Society gets the same EspoCRM tab out of the box.
  • In development
    A handled contact lands as a feedback record, with an agreed mapping of which contact fields flow into CFM.
  • Later
    The same connection to a CFM instance hosted by the National Society itself.

    How your National Society gets started

    You know your callers. We help you configure the system. No technical knowledge is needed.

    Setup workshop

    Half a day together. Four choices shape your contact centre: how many languages, how your team logs in, whether you record calls, and whether you send SMS.

    Write what callers hear

    Scenario by scenario, in your own words. Short, most important first, always a next step. You listen to every message in the computer voice.

    Test it yourselves

    510 tests a first version, then your team tests every scenario on a test number before anything goes live.

    Train, pilot, go live

    Agents and supervisors practise in the online training. You pilot in one area, then move your number over.
